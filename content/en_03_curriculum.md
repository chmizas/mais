## Program background

The postgraduate study program MSc in Information Systems is a continuation of the postgraduate study program "Management Science and Information Systems," which has been operating since 2011 with particular success.

## Full Time Program Overview

Students must participate in prescribed courses, exercises, seminars, research activities, pass examinations, and successfully defend a graduate thesis. The program requires 30 ECTS credits per semester, with the thesis worth 30 ECTS. Total requirement for the Master's degree: 90 ECTS credits.

### 1st Semester (30 ECTS total)
